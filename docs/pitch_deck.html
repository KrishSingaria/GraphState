Frequently Asked Questions
Q: Does the gate slow down the agent?
No. Verification takes <10 ms. By giving the agent upfront caller coordinates, it avoids trial-and-error thrashing, cutting complex refactor turns in half.
Q: What happens if the power cuts or engine crashes?
Disk is authoritative. The Write-Ahead Journal (WAL) restores half-written files on restart, while FalkorDB persists memories via AOF.
Q: When is GraphState best utilized?
Critical systems, automated pipelines, and shared libraries where unverified filesystem edits are unacceptable.
